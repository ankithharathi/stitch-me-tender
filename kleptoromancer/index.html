Preview

iPhone · Android · Desktop browser

You play the villain, and a dashing one: a mustache-twirling heart-thief working a derelict neon fairground at the end of the world.

Rival wranglers parade their mutant monsters on stage. Play charm cards to push each monster’s carnival love-tester into the pink sweet zone. Solid flames always land. Ghost flames flicker and land half the time.

Smooch at the right moment and the monster is yours while its wrangler sobs. Push too far and it chomps you.

Stolen monsters join your entourage and guard you. Trade their devotion at the capsule machine for spicier charms, then crash a wasteland wedding to steal the groom.

The whole game is wordless. Every stat is a flame, a heart or a sound.

How it plays

1Flirt

Charm cards heat up each monster’s love-tester. Matching its belly symbols adds a flame.

2Gamble

Ghost flames land half the time. Overshoot the pink zone and it chomps.

3Steal

Smooch inside the zone to steal it. Higher in the zone means more devotion.

4Collect

Spend devotion on capsules for spicier charms, then crash the wedding.
